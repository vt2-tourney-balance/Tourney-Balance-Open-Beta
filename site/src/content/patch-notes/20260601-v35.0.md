*With many of the changes affecting this mod from the recent balance update, why is TB not looking at the weapons that overlap with the patch's changes? Good question.* 

*Fatshark, in their infinite wisdom, did not provide the numbers they've changed in the patch notes yet the exact numerical differences are needed for us to accurately update the changelog now and in the future. Also, I am trying to keep things interesting and not ruin too much of the fun. If some overpowered interaction currently exists due to Fatshark's changes then you lot can enjoy that for a bit longer... until I come around with the hammer that is to make sure nobody ruins the upcoming tournament.*

**With the exception of urgent fixes, there will be no TB updates until the end of the Linesman Tourney**

# Helborg's Tutelage (Mercenary) - Updated Description
*I wrote this talent back in October last year and it has went through a few QoL changes added to it. The description was changed to simply be a carbon copy of Official's to keep it simple as possible whilst staying true to what the talent does.*
* Changed wording to better reflect how the talent actually functions now.

# Just Reward (Bounty Hunter)
*A talent that sees no use currently. I don't think this buff is enough but it's a start.*
* **20% Ult Cooldown gained on Ranged Crit:** Once every 10 seconds ⇒ **4.5 seconds**

# Moonfire Bow - Energy Recharge Nerf
*For a weapon that has infinite ammo, and considering the special pressure present in modded difficulties, its uptime was making the Longbow obsolete on every Elf career barring Waystalker. A later nerf is planned to increase the property investment required to reach certain breakpoints.*
* **Recharge rate:** 1.5 ⇒ **1**

# Piercing Shot (Waystalker) - QoL Fix
* Shooting through allies does not prevent cooldown refund from headshots.

# Lingering Flames (Battle Wizard) - Nerf
*When a Lingering build is played with Conflag and Kaboom for example, around 70% of overall dps comes from DoTs. This nerf effectively increases ttk on every enemy by roughly 30%, in turn putting a greater emphasis on using one's staff and melee weapon to deal more damage.*
* **DoT damage reduced:** 50% ⇒ **67%**

# Immersive Immolation (Battle Wizard) - Buff
*Another talent that saw no play time. As I've just nerfed Lingering I thought it prudent to give a build direction, or at least another option.*
* **Stat change:** Attack Speed ⇒ **Melee Attack Speed**
* **Hit Requirement:** 4 ⇒ **1**

# Hagbane Shortbow
*This nerf came about since Hagbane was outdoing BW in dps and it had become the most effective Stormvermin patrol-deleter in the game only outdone by things like a Strength-Bomb combo.*
**Charged Shot**
* Reduced Non-crit Armour damage on the explosion by **100%** (0.1 ⇒ 0)

# Soulstealer Staff - Special Breakpoints 
*This weapon is proving to be far more complicated to balance than expected. I'll start with changing **Death Ascendant** to give Ranged Damage *(IN THE BETA 28/05)*. With 10% Chaos + EP + 5 stacks, Death Ascendant with Ranged Damage grants an additional 1.22% damage. I highly doubt this'll be what breaks the camel's back.*

## Left-Click
* Reduced Non-Crit Armour damage by **6.25%** (0.8 ⇒ 0.75)
* Reduced Crit-Armour damage by **63%** (0.35 ⇒ 0.13)
* Reduced Crit-Berserker damage by **36%** (1 ⇒ 0.64)

## Right-Click (da suck)
* **Targeting Change:** Changed targeting to prioritise specials over elites. *(IN THE BETA 31/05)*
*Developer Note: Should make it easier to target Hookrats in a dense horde.*

**Breakpoints w/ Chaos, Skaven, 2xInfantry + EP:**
* **Blightstormer, Leech, Assassin & Hookrat** ⇒ 1x Suck
* **Flamer/Gunner** ⇒ 3x Stack (Ascendant) / WHC Tag / Necro Passive (20% extra damage against burning stuff) + 1x Suck
* **Mauler** ⇒ 2x Left-Click (bodyshot) + Suck

**Worth Noting:**
* **Lowered Super-Armour damage:** CW & Standard Bearer cannot be just removed from the game.
* **Low Berserker damage:** The weapon needed an enemy type to be weak against. Considering Sienna's melee arsenal, having a ranged weapon not do so well against Berserkers/Monks, and Nurgloth, seems fine... I think.
