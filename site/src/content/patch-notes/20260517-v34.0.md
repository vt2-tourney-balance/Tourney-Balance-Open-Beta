# Mercenary
*Strike Together was made into a passive for Mercenary to make him more competitive against the other Kruber careers.*

**Paced Strikes (Passive):**
* Hitting 3 enemies in one swing grants 10% increased attack speed for 6 seconds **to all players**.

**Strike Together:**
* Proccing *Paced Strikes* now requires hitting only one enemy.

**Helborg's Tutelage:**
* Now properly gives random crits. Additionally, the guaranteed critical hit is no longer consumed if the player misses the attack.

# Blunderbuss, Grudge-Raker & Griffon-Foot
*Bashes are AoE attacks that enabled the rapid regeneration of temporary health and ult charge. The risk associated with making use of this interaction became arbitrary compared to the value a player was able to extract. It was simply too effective. Nerfing the radius is a brute force method that I will take further if needed.*

**Bash:**
* Radius reduced by **64%** (2.5 ➔ 0.9)
