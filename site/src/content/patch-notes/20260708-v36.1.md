**Boon of Shallya**
- Adjustment: 30% -> 40%
*Barkskin is still considered generally superior. This buff allows for more build variety.*

**Dissipate**
- Adjustment: Reduced the heat cost of blocking attacks by 80%.
*This talent enabled a gameplay loop with zero downtime. You shot with your staff, at high heat you'd go into melee and when you eventually block an attack you'd have your heat vented for free then you'd get to use your staff again. The nerf allows UC to retain the increased BCR for a longer period of time whilst reducing staff uptime.*
