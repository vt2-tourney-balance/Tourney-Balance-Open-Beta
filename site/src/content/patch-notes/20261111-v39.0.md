# v39.0 - Open Beta

## Outcast Engineer
**Build Pressure (Passive)**
* Stacks are now lost 1 at a time.

**Steam-Assisted Crank Gun (Mk II) (Ultimate)**
* Crank gun also starts at full rotation speed while aiming with right click.
* Aimed shots with right click are now pinpoint accurate
* Aimed shots have damage falloff range increased to 30-50 (from 10-20). Same as official realm handgun.

**Ingenious Ordnance**
* Reduced bomb craft timer to 60s (from 80s).

**Armour Piercing Slugs**
* Non-explosive ranged attacks no longer deal friendly fire.

**Bombardier**
* Increase bomb pocket size to 6 (from 3).
* Increase starting bombs to 6 (from 3).

**Superior Gaskets**
* Increased time until stacks are lost to 3s (from 1.5s)

**Ablative Armor**
* Stacks are lost 5s after taking damage.

**Combined Arms**
* The next ranged attack also makes non-explosive ranged attacks consume no ammo for 10s.

**Piston Power**
* For each stamina shield lost, reduces cooldown of Piston Power by 1 seconds.
