## Pickaxe
*Fatshark's latest changes led to crit damage on Uncharged and Charged Heavies to deal less crit damage than their non-crit counterparts. These have very high base damage so I went for somewhat conservative crit multipliers for them. No longer crashes.*

**Uncharged Heavies:**
* Crit Armor Multiplier increased by **94%** (`0.5` ➔ `0.947`)
* Crit Super Armor Multiplier increased by **94%** (`0.5` ➔ `0.947`)

**Charged Heavies:**
* Crit Armor Multiplier increased by **57%** (`0.5` ➔ `0.785`)
* Crit Super Armor Multiplier increased by **57%** (`0.5` ➔ `0.785`)
