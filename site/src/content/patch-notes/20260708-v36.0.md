**Removed Stam-Tech**
*It could be argued that Stam-tech is similar to QQ-cancelling. That would be a fair point. However, the benefit brought by these two acts is in different leagues. Stam-tech allows a frontliner to never have to worry about managing their stamina. It removes the skill needed in its entirety yet the effort required is disproportionately low.*

**1H Axe (Bardin/Victor) Cleave Buff**:
- Increased `cleave_distribution.attack` and `cleave_distribution.impact`
```
L1, L2:  0.075 ➔ 0.15
L3:      0.075 ➔ 0.3
```
*Makes dealing with Slaverats, Clanrats and Fanatics slightly less cumbersome. It's the sort of change that smoothens out melee gameplay, makes it flow better. It's effectively a QoL change. A surprisingly intelligent change from Fatshark. Will be tweaked, if it's too OP.*


**Fervent Huntress**
- (experimental) Added noclip
*A talent like this which sees no use yet has strong competition within its row gets to experience our version of artistic liberty. Noclip is a powerful interaction as it enables a player to reposition with far greater ease and opens up a more avenues for skill expression.*
