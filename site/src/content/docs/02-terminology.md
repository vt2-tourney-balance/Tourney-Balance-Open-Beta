## Careers

Each hero has four careers. Career names used across this site match the
mod's internal naming convention.

### Career prefixes

- `es_` — Empire Soldier (Kruber)
- `dr_` — Dwarf Ranger (Bardin)
- `we_` — Wood Elf (Kerillian)
- `wh_` — Witch Hunter (Victor)
- `bw_` — Bright Wizard (Sienna)

## Talents

Each career has six talent rows, with a choice of 3 talents per row.

## Traits

Traits are bonus effects attached to weapons and equipment, separate from
base stats and properties.
