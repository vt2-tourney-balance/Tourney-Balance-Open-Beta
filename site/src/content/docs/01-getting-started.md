## What is Tourney Balance

Tourney Balance is a competitive balance mod for Warhammer: Vermintide 2,
maintained for the tournament and modded scene.

## Installing the mod

Subscribe to the mod on the Steam Workshop and enable it from mod manager in the game launcher.

### Open Beta vs Official

There are two versions of this mod — Official (stable) and Open Beta
(testing upcoming changes before they're merged into Official).

## Where changes are documented

- **Patch Notes** — developer commentary on what changed and why, in
  chronological order.

- **Balance Changes** — a structured, browsable breakdown of every
  Career/Talent/Weapon/Misc change relative to official balance. (WIP)
